About us

A little home kitchen with a big heart

Golden Crumb began on a family kitchen counter, baking birthday cakes for neighbours. Today we still bake every order by hand, in small batches, from scratch, with the same butter, patience and care.

No factory trays and no preservatives. Whether it is a custom celebration cake, a box of fudgy brownies or a basket of warm buns, we bake it the morning you collect it, so it reaches your table at its very best.
